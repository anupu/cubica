# Magic and RPG Mods

This pack has a rich magic system built around spells, enchantments, and exploration.
Think of it as a full RPG layer sitting on top of your Minecraft world.

---

## Iron's Spellbooks

**What is it?**
Iron's Spellbooks adds a complete magic system to the game. You collect spell scrolls,
copy them into a spell book, and cast them using mana. Spells range from fireballs and
lightning to healing, summoning undead, and teleportation. Each spell levels up the
more you use it, making your mage build feel genuinely progressive.

**How to get started:**

1. Find a **Ruined Book** or loot **Spell Scrolls** from dungeons, chests, and mobs.
   Scrolls drop from a wide variety of enemies, so keep an eye out early on.

2. Craft an **Arcane Salvager's Workbench** to break down duplicate scrolls into
   **Arcane Essence**, the main crafting currency for magic gear.

3. Craft a **Leather Spell Book** as your first spell book. It holds a handful of spells
   and is cheap to make.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Leather Spell Book crafting recipe in EMI/inventory -->
   <!-- Find at: https://ironsspellbooks.com or search "Iron's Spellbooks wiki" -->
   ![Leather Spell Book Recipe](PLACEHOLDER_leather_spell_book_recipe.png)

4. Right-click the spell book to open it, then insert scrolls to learn spells.
   Equip the spell book to your offhand (or mainhand) and press **R** (default) to cast.

5. Craft or find **Magic Robes** for stat bonuses. Early robes can be found in loot or
   crafted from wool and arcane essence.

**Key progression items:**

| Item                        | What it does                                           |
|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Spell Scroll                | Teaches you one spell when inserted into a spell book  |
| Arcane Essence              | Core crafting material for all magic gear              |
| Spell Book (Leather -> Netherite tiers) | More spell slots and mana as you upgrade   |
| Magic Robes                 | Armor that boosts spell power, mana, and cast speed    |
| Mana Flask                  | Restores mana on the go                                |

> **Pro tip:** Your mana regenerates over time. Early on, bring Mana Flasks on adventures
> so you are never stuck unable to cast. The Restoration spell is a fantastic first pick
> if you find the scroll -- free healing is incredibly useful.

## Apotheosis

**What is it?**
Apotheosis massively overhauls enchanting, spawners, bosses, and loot. The part most
relevant to you as a new player is the **enchanting overhaul** -- it replaces the vanilla
enchanting table with a much more powerful and flexible system.

**How to get started (Enchanting):**

1. Place your vanilla **Enchanting Table** as usual. Apotheosis upgrades it automatically.

2. Craft and place **Bookshelves** around it like normal, but now you can also place
   special **Apotheosis shelves** (Hellshelf, Crystalshelf, etc.) for extra power and
   to unlock more enchantment options.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: An Apotheosis-enhanced enchanting table setup with various shelves -->
   <!-- Find at: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apotheosis -->
   ![Apotheosis Enchanting Setup](PLACEHOLDER_apotheosis_enchanting_setup.png)

3. You can now apply enchantments **one at a time** instead of rolling random combos.
   Use **Arcane Dust** (found in loot) to guarantee specific enchantments.

4. An **Anvil** still works for combining books, but Apotheosis extends max enchantment
   levels well beyond vanilla limits.

**What else does Apotheosis add?** (explore these later!)
- **Spawner rework:** Break and move spawners, modify what they spawn.
- **Affix loot:** Rare drops with random bonus effects (like "deals extra fire damage").
- **Bosses:** Powerful named enemies with unique loot scattered around the world.

> **Pro tip:** The Apotheosis enchanting system opens up when you bring in shelves from
> the Nether or beyond. Push into the Nether early to unlock better enchantment options.

## The Aether

**What is it?**
The Aether is a sky dimension -- the opposite of the Nether. It is a bright, floating
island world full of unique mobs, dungeons, loot, and its own progression path. Think of
it as a "second early-mid game" to run alongside or after the Nether.

**How to get started:**

1. Craft a **Glowstone Frame** portal (same shape as a Nether portal, but use Glowstone
   blocks instead of Obsidian).

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Aether portal frame made of Glowstone, unlit -->
   <!-- Find at: https://aether.wiki.gg -->
   ![Aether Portal Frame](PLACEHOLDER_aether_portal_frame.png)

2. Light it with a **bucket of water** (right-click the inside of the frame with water).
   Step through!

3. In the Aether you will find:
   - **Skyroot Trees** -- your first wood source up there
   - **Aerclouds** -- bouncy cloud blocks
   - **Moa eggs** -- hatch a Moa for a flying mount (with the **Protect Your Moa** addon,
     your Moa can be better protected, so treat it well!)
   - **Bronze, Zanite, and Gravitite** -- the Aether's own ore progression

4. Tackle **Bronze Dungeons** (small, with the Slider boss) first for loot, then move
   up to **Silver Dungeons** (Valkyrie Queen) and **Gold Dungeons** (Sun Spirit).

**Key Aether items:**

| Item             | What it does                                           |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Skyroot Sword    | Faster attack speed, your first Aether weapon          |
| Zanite Gemstone  | Mid-tier Aether gem, used for Zanite tools and armor   |
| Gravitite Ore    | Top-tier Aether material, can enchant to float items!  |
| Aether Boots     | Let you float upward briefly -- excellent for climbing |
| Ambrosium Torch  | The Aether's light source                              |

> **Pro tip:** The Aether has its own enchanting system using an **Altar**. You can
> enchant items here in addition to the Apotheosis table. Bring healing items --
> the dungeons are tough!

## Other Magic Mods

This pack has several more magic mods worth knowing about. They are a bit more niche
but add great depth once you are comfortable with Iron's Spellbooks and Apotheosis.

**Darker Magic**
Adds dark and shadow magic spells and rituals. Look for **Darker Magic tomes** in
dungeons and dark structures. Great for players who want a more sinister magic style.

**Discerning the Eldritch**
An eldritch / cosmic horror themed magic mod. Adds strange artifacts and spells tied
to ancient, unknowable power. Find its content in rare structures and deep underground.

**Thaumon**
A thaumaturgy-inspired magic mod. Expect research, wands, and a rune-based crafting
system. More complex -- save this for when you have a base established.

> These mods pair naturally with Iron's Spellbooks. Build your core spell loadout first,
> then branch into these for extra flavor and power.

# Tech and Automation Mods

The tech side of this pack is centered on **Create**, one of the most popular and
visually satisfying automation mods ever made. It is paired with Applied Energistics 2
for storage and Immersive Engineering for an industrial feel.

---

## Create

**What is it?**
Create is a mechanical engineering mod built around **rotation and motion**. Instead of
just placing machines and connecting them with cables, you build physical contraptions
using gears, shafts, belts, and moving parts. Factories in Create look alive -- they
spin, move, and animate in real time.

**How to get started:**

1. Your first goal is to make a **Mechanical Press** setup. You will need:
   - **Copper** and **Zinc** to craft **Brass** (using the Mixer or other means)
   - A **Water Wheel** or **Windmill Bearing** for your first rotation source

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: A basic Water Wheel connected via shaft to a Mechanical Press -->
   <!-- Find at: https://create.fandom.com/wiki/Create_Wiki -->
   ![Basic Water Wheel Setup](PLACEHOLDER_create_water_wheel.png)

2. A **Water Wheel** generates rotation (Stress Units / SU) when water flows over it.
   Place it in a river or set up water sources on both sides.

3. Connect the Water Wheel to a **Mechanical Press** using **Shafts** and **Gearboxes**.
   The Press can turn iron ingots into sheets and other materials you will need constantly.

4. Next, work toward a **Basin + Mixer** combo for alloy crafting (combining metals),
   and a **Depot + Funnel** setup for automated item movement.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: A Basin with a Mechanical Mixer above it, making Brass -->
   <!-- Find at: https://create.fandom.com/wiki/Brass -->
   ![Mechanical Mixer and Basin](PLACEHOLDER_create_mixer_basin.png)

**The core Create loop:**
Rotation Source -> Shafts/Gears -> Machines -> Products -> Storage

**Key early machines:**

| Machine              | What it does                                           |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Water Wheel          | Generates rotation from flowing water                  |
| Mechanical Press     | Presses ingots into sheets, stamps items               |
| Mechanical Mixer     | Mixes materials in a Basin to create alloys            |
| Deployer             | A robotic arm that "uses" items on things              |
| Chute / Funnel       | Moves items between machines automatically             |
| Millstone            | Grinds materials (wheat to flour, ores to powder)      |
| Portable Storage Interface | Lets trains and contraptions load/unload items  |

**Create addons in this pack:**
- **Create: Aeronautics / Aeroworks** -- Build flying machines and airships!
- **Create: Big Cannons** -- Working artillery. Enough said.
- **Create: New Age** -- Electricity and generators to power Create machines.
- **Create: Diesel Generators** -- Fuel-powered rotation source.
- **Create: Railways Navigator** -- Full train network with a route planner UI.
- **Create: Nuclear** -- Late-game nuclear power generation.
- **Create: Enchantable Machinery** -- Enchant your Create machines for bonuses.

> **Pro tip:** The in-game guide book is called the **Ponder** system. Hold a Create
> item and press **W** (default) to open an animated tutorial for that exact block.
> It is genuinely one of the best in-game tutorials in any mod -- use it constantly!

## Applied Energistics 2

**What is it?**
Applied Energistics 2 (AE2) is a storage and automation mod. It lets you store
**thousands of items in a single block** and interact with them through terminals.
Think of it as a digital warehouse for your entire base. Late-game it can auto-craft
almost anything on demand.

**How to get started:**

1. First you need **Certus Quartz** -- a new ore added by AE2, found underground.
   Mine it like normal ore.

2. Craft a **Charger** and power it (it can be powered by a Create rotation source
   early on). Use it to charge **Certus Quartz Crystals** into **Charged Certus Quartz**.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: AE2 Charger block with a Certus Quartz Crystal inside -->
   <!-- Find at: https://appliedenergistics.org -->
   ![AE2 Charger](PLACEHOLDER_ae2_charger.png)

3. Craft a **Meteorite Compass** to track down **Sky Stone Meteorites** -- these have
   the **Press** components you need to craft the core AE2 parts.

4. Once you have Presses, craft:
   - **ME Drive** -- the storage unit (holds storage cells)
   - **ME Controller** -- the brain of your network
   - **ME Terminal** -- your access point to everything stored

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: A minimal AE2 network: Controller + Drive + Terminal side by side -->
   <!-- Find at: https://appliedenergistics.org -->
   ![Basic AE2 Network](PLACEHOLDER_ae2_basic_network.png)

**Key AE2 concepts:**

| Term              | What it means                                          |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| ME Network        | Your connected grid of AE2 blocks                      |
| Storage Cell       | A card you insert into an ME Drive to hold items       |
| Channels          | Each cable can carry a limited number of devices       |
| Autocrafting      | Set up patterns so the network crafts items for you    |
| P2P Tunnel        | Lets you send items/channels through walls cleanly     |

> **Pro tip:** AE2 feels overwhelming at first. Just start with a Controller + Drive +
> Terminal. You do not need autocrafting right away -- even just having one searchable
> chest for all your items is a massive quality of life upgrade.

## Immersive Engineering

**What is it?**
Immersive Engineering (IE) adds industrial-age machinery with a realistic, gritty
aesthetic. Multi-block structures like blast furnaces, ore crushers, and wire-based
power grids. It pairs beautifully with Create for a full industrial base feel.

**How to get started:**

1. Craft the **Engineer's Handbook** -- your in-game guide to everything IE has to offer.
   Read it! It is well written and explains the progression clearly.

2. Your first goal is the **Crusher** (a multi-block ore processor that doubles your
   ore output) and the **Coke Oven** (produces Coke Coal and Creosote Oil as fuel).

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Immersive Engineering Coke Oven multi-block structure -->
   <!-- Find at: https://immersive-engineering.com or the IE wiki -->
   ![IE Coke Oven](PLACEHOLDER_ie_coke_oven.png)

3. Use **Wirecoils** to connect IE machines to power sources (like the Kinetic Dynamo,
   which converts Create rotation into IE power).

**Key IE items:**

| Item / Structure   | What it does                                           |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Coke Oven          | Turns coal into Coke Coal (better fuel) + Creosote Oil |
| Crusher            | Doubles ore output by crushing ores into grit          |
| Arc Furnace        | High-end smelter for alloys and bulk processing        |
| Railgun / Revolver | Powerful unique weapons available through IE           |
| Conveyor Belt      | IE's own item transport system                         |

> **Pro tip:** IE and Create complement each other very well -- Create moves things
> physically, IE provides industrial power and processing. Consider running both in
> your main factory.

# Food and Farming Mods

Eating in this pack is way more interesting than vanilla. The food mods add hundreds
of recipes and ingredients, and Mystical Agriculture turns farming into a full
progression system.

---

## Farmer's Delight

**What is it?**
Farmer's Delight is the foundation of the food system in this pack. It adds cooking
mechanics (a **Cooking Pot** and **Cutting Board**), dozens of new crops, and meals
that give powerful buffs. Many other mods in this pack add their own Farmer's Delight
recipes and ingredients on top.

**How to get started:**

1. Craft a **Cutting Board** first -- it lets you process raw ingredients (chop
   vegetables, cut meat into smaller pieces) using a knife.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Cutting Board recipe and example of chopping a vegetable on it -->
   <!-- Find at: https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/wiki -->
   ![Farmer's Delight Cutting Board](PLACEHOLDER_fd_cutting_board.png)

2. Craft a **Straw** from wheat, then a **Knife** (any metal ingot + stick). The knife
   is your main tool for the Cutting Board.

3. Craft a **Cooking Pot** and place it over a heat source (fire, campfire, or a
   Create burner). Right-click to open it and place ingredients inside.
   A **Bowl** in the output slot starts cooking!

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Cooking Pot over a campfire with ingredients inside and a bowl ready -->
   <!-- Find at: https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/wiki -->
   ![Cooking Pot in Action](PLACEHOLDER_fd_cooking_pot.png)

4. Grow the new Farmer's Delight crops -- **Tomatoes, Cabbages, Onions, Rice,** and
   more. They grow like vanilla crops on farmland.

**Key meals and what they do:**

| Meal                  | Key buff                                               |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Vegetable Soup        | Solid early-game food, easy to make                    |
| Roasted Mutton Chops  | High saturation, great for combat runs                 |
| Stew / Noodle Soup    | Regeneration effect                                    |
| Stuffed Pumpkin       | Long-lasting buffs, great for exploring                |
| Honey Glazed Ham      | Resistance buff -- fantastic before a dungeon dive     |

**Farmer's Delight addons in this pack:**
This pack stacks a huge amount of food addons on top of Farmer's Delight:
Cultural Delights, Ocean's Delight, Aquaculture Delight, Barbeques Delight,
Chef's Delight, Brewin and Chewin (drinks!), Vinery, and many more.
Use **EMI** (press **E** in your inventory) to browse all the recipes -- there are
hundreds of dishes to discover!

> **Pro tip:** Food buffs stack. A good pre-dungeon meal combo (Honey Glazed Ham +
> a soup with Regeneration) can make boss fights much more survivable. Cook in bulk
> and keep your inventory stocked.

## Mystical Agriculture

**What is it?**
Mystical Agriculture lets you grow almost any resource as a crop -- ores, mob drops,
even rare materials like diamonds and netherite. It replaces grinding for materials
with patient, scalable farming. A fully set-up Mystical Agriculture farm can supply
your entire base with resources passively.

**How to get started:**

1. First, craft **Prosperity Shards** (found in loot or crafted) and **Inferium Essence**,
   which you get by mining **Inferium Ore** found underground.

2. Use Inferium Essence to craft **Inferium Seeds** and plant them on regular farmland.
   They drop more Inferium Essence when harvested.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Inferium Ore block in world and Inferium Seeds on farmland -->
   <!-- Find at: https://wiki.curseforge.com/mystical-agriculture -->
   ![Inferium Ore and Seeds](PLACEHOLDER_ma_inferium.png)

3. Upgrade Inferium Essence through the four tiers:
   **Inferium -> Prudentium -> Tertium -> Imperium -> Supremium**
   Each tier is crafted from the tier below in a 3x3 pattern.

4. Craft **Resource Seeds** using Prosperity Shards + Essence + a material from that
   resource (e.g., an Iron Ingot for Iron Seeds). Plant on **Essence Farmland** (crafted
   from the relevant tier of Essence).

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Iron Seeds planted on Essence Farmland, fully grown -->
   <!-- Find at: https://wiki.curseforge.com/mystical-agriculture -->
   ![Iron Seeds on Essence Farmland](PLACEHOLDER_ma_iron_seeds.png)

5. Automate harvesting with a Create **Mechanical Harvester** on a moving contraption
   for a fully hands-off resource farm!

**Essence tier overview:**

| Tier        | What you can grow                                      |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Inferium    | The base essence -- gather this first                  |
| Prudentium  | Unlock Stone, Dirt, Wood seeds                         |
| Tertium     | Unlock Iron, Copper, Coal seeds                        |
| Imperium    | Unlock Gold, Lapis, Redstone seeds                     |
| Supremium   | Unlock Diamond, Emerald, Netherite, and more!          |

> **Pro tip:** Mystical Agriculture is a long-term investment. Set up a small Inferium
> farm early, leave it running, and by mid-game you will have enough essence to start
> crafting powerful resource seeds. It pairs brilliantly with Create automation.

## Deeper and Darker

**What is it?**
Deeper and Darker expands the Deep Dark -- the eerie, sculk-covered underground biome
from vanilla Minecraft 1.19+. If you found Ancient Cities creepy and exciting, this
mod adds more of everything: more structures, new mobs, new gear, and a new dimension
hidden beneath the deep dark itself.

**How to get started:**

1. Find the **Deep Dark** biome far underground (it generates below Y=0, usually under
   mountains). You will know it by the sculk carpet and the oppressive darkness.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: A Deep Dark biome with sculk blocks and shriekers visible -->
   <!-- Find at: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/deeperdarker -->
   ![Deep Dark Biome](PLACEHOLDER_dd_deep_dark.png)

2. Explore **Ancient Cities** -- Deeper and Darker adds new loot and structures here.
   Sneak carefully around **Sculk Shriekers** to avoid summoning the Warden!

3. Look for the new **Otherside Portal** hidden in the deepest Ancient City structures.
   It leads to the new **Otherside Dimension** -- Deeper and Darker's main new area.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: The Otherside portal structure inside an Ancient City -->
   <!-- Find at: https://deeperdarker.wiki.gg -->
   ![Otherside Portal](PLACEHOLDER_dd_otherside_portal.png)

4. Collect **Sculk Jaw**, **Warden-related drops**, and new Deeper and Darker materials
   to craft the mod's unique armor and tools -- among the most powerful in the pack.

**Key Deeper and Darker items:**

| Item                  | What it does                                           |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Warden Armor          | Powerful armor with sculk-themed effects               |
| Sculk Bone            | Crafting material dropped by Deep Dark mobs            |
| Echo Shard (expanded) | Used for crafting and portal activation                |
| Shrieker's Tooth      | New weapon material from the Deep Dark                 |

> **Warning:** The Warden is still here and still terrifying. Sneaking is not optional.
> Bring lots of torches -- the Deep Dark is one of the darkest places in the game.

## The Undergarden

**What is it?**
The Undergarden is a vast underground dimension sitting far below the bedrock layer.
Unlike the Nether, it is lush, dark, and dripping with atmosphere -- alien mushroom
forests, glowing plants, toxic bogs, and strange new creatures. It has its own ore
progression and is a fantastic exploration destination in mid-game.

**How to get started:**

1. Craft an **Undergarden Catalyst** using materials gathered from the overworld.
   You will need **Gloomslate** (the Undergarden's stone, occasionally found as rare
   veins near bedrock in the overworld) and other materials.

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: Undergarden Catalyst crafting recipe -->
   <!-- Find at: https://the-undergarden.fandom.com/wiki -->
   ![Undergarden Catalyst Recipe](PLACEHOLDER_ug_catalyst_recipe.png)

2. Place the Catalyst to open a **portal to the Undergarden** and step through.
   The dimension is pitch dark -- bring plenty of torches or a light source!

3. Gather **Gloomslate** and the Undergarden's unique ores:
   - **Depthrock** -- the base stone of the dimension
   - **Cloggrum, Froststeel, Utherium** -- the Undergarden's metal progression

   <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
   <!-- Screenshot: The Undergarden dimension with glowing mushrooms and dark stone -->
   <!-- Find at: https://the-undergarden.fandom.com/wiki -->
   ![The Undergarden Dimension](PLACEHOLDER_ug_dimension.png)

4. Watch out for the dimension's unique hostile mobs -- they hit hard and spawn
   aggressively in the dark. Keep your area lit up as you explore.

**Key Undergarden items:**

| Item           | What it does                                             |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Cloggrum       | First Undergarden metal tier, makes solid early tools    |
| Froststeel     | Mid-tier, has frost/chill effects on tools and armor     |
| Utherium       | Top-tier Undergarden metal, exceptional stats            |
| Forgotten Ingot | Rare powerful material found in Undergarden ruins       |
| Glowing Ink Sac | Dropped by squid variants, useful for glowing signs    |

> **Pro tip:** The Undergarden is best explored in mid-game when you have strong armor
> and a reliable light source. Bring food -- the dimension does not have a day/night
> cycle and mobs spawn constantly. A Create lantern contraption to light your path
> as you move is incredibly satisfying here!
