# Magic and RPG Mods

This pack has a rich magic system built around spells, enchantments, and exploration.
Think of it as a full RPG layer sitting on top of your Minecraft world.

---

## Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks

**What is it?**
Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks adds a complete magic system with over 100 spells across
nine schools of magic: Fire, Ice, Blood, Ender, Evocation, Lightning, Holy, Nature,
and Void. You collect spell scrolls, inscribe them into a spell book, and cast them
using mana. Each spell has multiple levels and levels up as you upgrade your equipment
and gear -- making your mage build feel genuinely progressive, not just loot-dependent.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Start exploring immediately. Spell Scrolls and Arcane Essence drop from loot chests
      in structures and from hostile mobs across the world. The mod adds seven unique
      structures -- look out for **Wizard Towers** and similar builds -- and killing the
      wizard enemies inside is one of the best early sources of scrolls and gear.

   2. Once you have a spell scroll, craft an **Inscription Table**. This
      workstation is where you slot scrolls into spell books to learn spells.

      ![1777665124581](image/cubica-intro/1777665124581.png)

   3. Craft your first spell book. The cheapest is the **Flimsy Journal** or the
      **Ironbound Tome** -- these hold a limited number of spell slots. Spellbooks
      follow vanilla tiers (Iron, Diamond, Netherite) as you progress, with higher
      tiers holding more spells and providing more mana.

   4. Equip the spell book in your main hand. Use **V** (default keybind) to cast
      the currently selected spell. Change your spells using **R** (default keybind).
      You can also equip a **Staff** in your main hand and right-click to cast, freeing
      up your off-hand for a shield or another item (or vice versa).

   5. Once you have enough **Arcane Essence**, craft the **Wandering Magician** armor
      set (leather armor combined with Arcane Essence). It is cheap and gives meaningful
      mana and spell power bonuses right away -- a big upgrade from nothing.

   6. Mid-game, unlock the **Arcane Anvil** workstation. It lets you:
      - Combine two identical scrolls of the same level to create a higher-level version
      - Bind spells directly onto weapons (sword with a Fireball charge, for example)
      - Upgrade your spell slot capacity with **Lesser Spell Slot Upgrades**

   7. For ink and late-game scroll crafting, use the **Alchemist Cauldron** (place it
      over a campfire, add water, toss in unwanted spells to brew inks). Inks are used
      in the **Scroll Forge** to craft custom scrolls of your chosen school.

   **The three progression phases:**

   | Phase        | Goal                                                             |
   |--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
   | Early        | Explore structures, loot scrolls and Arcane Essence             |
   | Mid-game     | Build an Arcane Anvil, upgrade spell levels, brew ink           |
   | Late-game    | Defeat bosses (the Dead King, Fire Boss), unlock legendary gear |

   **Key items:**

   | Item                    | What it does                                            |
   |-------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
   | Spell Scroll            | Contains one spell; inscribe it into a book to use it   |
   | Arcane Essence          | Core crafting material for all magic gear               |
   | Inscription Table       | Workstation for slotting scrolls into spell books       |
   | Arcane Anvil            | Upgrades spells, binds spells to weapons                |
   | Alchemist Cauldron      | Brews inks from spells for late-game scroll crafting    |
   | Wandering Magician Set  | Cheap early armor with mana and spell power bonuses     |
   | Staff                   | Cast spells by right-clicking; frees your offhand       |
   | School-specific Armor   | 10 armor sets, each boosting a specific magic school    |

   **Wiki:** [https://iron.wiki/](https://iron.wiki/)

   > **Pro tip:** There is an in-game guide book for this mod accessible via Patchouli.
   > Run the command `/give @p patchouli:guide_book[patchouli:book="irons_spellbooks:iss_guide_book"]`
   > in chat to receive it. It covers everything from spell schools to progression steps
   > and is well worth reading early on.
</details>

## Apotheosis

**What is it?**
Apotheosis overhauls several vanilla systems and adds new RPG-style loot layers.
Its modules cover enchanting, spawners, potions, and a full adventure/affix system.
For new players, the most immediately impactful parts are the **enchanting overhaul**
and the **Affix loot** system -- the latter makes exploring actually rewarding by
adding randomized powerful bonuses to the gear you find.

Get the in-game guide by crafting a **Chronicle of Shadows** (a Book and a Gold
Ingot). Read it! It covers everything and updates based on what is enabled in your
modpack.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **Enchanting overhaul:**

   Apotheosis replaces the vanilla "roll a random combo" enchanting with a three-stat
   system. Instead of one slot that takes levels, you now see three values:

   - **Eterna** -- equivalent to vanilla enchanting power (more bookshelves = more Eterna)
   - **Quanta** -- increases the variability of enchantments (higher risk, higher reward)
   - **Arcana** -- unlocks rarer and more powerful enchantment options

   The vanilla Enchanting Table goes all the way to level 150 with the right shelves
   surrounding it. You can also pick enchantments individually, and the Anvil cap has
   been removed.

   **Shelf progression** (place these around your enchanting table for more power):

   Start with vanilla Bookshelves, then progress through Nether-material shelves like
   **Hellshelf** and **Seashelf**, all the way to endgame **Draconic Endshelf** for
   the maximum possible enchanting setup.

   **Affix loot:**

   Any piece of gear you find in the world now has a chance to be an Affix Item -- a
   colored, named drop with randomized bonuses like "deals fire damage on hit" or
   "grants extra movement speed." These come in five rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare,
   Epic, and Mythic. Items also have **Gem Sockets** that you can fill with socketed
   Gems dropped by hostile mobs for further bonuses.

   Break down Affix Items you do not want in a **Salvaging Table** to get Rarity
   Materials, which you then use at a **Reforging Table** to roll your own Affix Items.

   **World Tiers and Bosses:**

   As you explore, the world progresses through World Tiers (view/ select: CTRL + T). 
   ![1777667767190](image/cubica-intro/1777667767190.png)
   From Tier 1 (Frontier) onwards, **Apothic Invaders** start appearing -- empowered named enemies that drop
   guaranteed high-quality Affix Items. They are announced by a beam of light from the
   sky and a loud sound. Defeating your first Invader is required to advance to the next
   World Tier.

   **What else does Apotheosis add?**
   - **Spawner rework:** Pick up spawners with Silk Touch. Modify their spawn rate,
   mobs, and conditions. Extremely powerful for mob farms.
   - **New potions:** New brews with effects not found in vanilla.
   - **Cobwebs** remove enchantments from items; **Prismatic Cobwebs** remove curses.

   > **Pro tip:** Do not throw away colored Affix Items even if the stats are bad.
   > Salvage them at the Salvaging Table for Rarity Materials -- you will need these
   > for the Reforging Table later, and they stack up quickly if you save them from
   > the start.
</details>

## Other Magic Mods

This pack has several more magic mods worth knowing about. They are a bit more niche
but add great depth once you are comfortable with Iron's Spellbooks and Apotheosis.

**Darker Magic**
Adds shadow and dark magic spells and rituals. Pairs naturally with the Void and
Blood schools from Iron's Spellbooks. Look for Darker Magic tomes in dungeons and
dark structures. A great pick if you want a more sinister caster build.

**Discerning the Eldritch**
A cosmic horror themed magic mod with strange artifacts and eldritch spells. Finds
its content in rare structures and deep underground ruins. More complex but very
rewarding to explore.

**Thaumon**
A thaumaturgy-inspired magic mod with wands, rune-based crafting, and a research
system. More involved than the others -- save this for mid-game when your base is
established.

> These mods pair naturally with Iron's Spellbooks. Build your core spell loadout
> and get comfortable with mana management first, then branch into these for
> extra flavor and additional power sources.

# Tech and Automation Mods

The tech side of this pack is centered on **Create**, one of the most visually
satisfying and mechanically unique automation mods ever made. It is paired with
Applied Energistics 2 for mass storage and Immersive Engineering for an industrial
aesthetic and additional machines.

---

## Create

**What is it?**
Create is a mechanical engineering mod built around **rotation and motion**. Instead
of placing machines and connecting them with cables like most tech mods, you build
physical contraptions using gears, shafts, belts, and moving parts. Factories in
Create look alive -- they spin, move, and animate in real time. Almost everything
runs on **Rotational Force**, measured in **Stress Units (SU)** and **RPM**.

**The core concept:**
Every Create system has two sides: **Generators** that provide Stress Capacity
(like a Water Wheel), and **Consumers** that use Stress Impact (like a Mechanical
Press). As long as your total Stress Capacity is greater than or equal to your total
Stress Impact, the whole network runs. If you add too many machines without adding
more generators, the system will **overstress and stop entirely**.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Your first task is making **Brass**, the core Create material. Brass is made by
      mixing Copper and Zinc in a **Mechanical Mixer** over a **Basin**. But to run the
      Mixer, you need a rotation source first.

   2. Build a **Water Wheel** for your first rotation source. Place it in a river, or
      set up two water sources on opposite sides of it. The direction of water flow
      determines the direction of rotation -- connect it to your machines using
      **Shafts** and **Gearboxes**.

      ![1777668106057](image/cubica-intro/1777668106057.png)

   3. Connect the Water Wheel to a **Mechanical Press** via Shafts. The Press is your
      most important early machine -- it presses Iron Ingots into Sheets, which are
      required for almost every Create recipe.

   4. Next, work toward the **Mixer + Basin** combo for smelting Brass. Once you have
      Brass, the whole mod opens up: faster transport (Belts), better machines, and
      eventually trains, flying machines, and much more.

   **Useful speed tips:**
   - Use **Small Cogwheels on Large Cogwheels** to double rotation speed (but also
   doubles Stress Impact on connected machines)
   - Use **Large Cogwheels on Small Cogwheels** to halve speed (and halve stress)
   - A **Rotation Speed Controller** lets you set an exact RPM -- very useful

   **Key early machines:**

   | Machine                 | What it does                                            |
   |-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
   | Water Wheel             | Generates rotation from flowing water                   |
   | Windmill Bearing        | Generates rotation from sails in open air (more power)  |
   | Mechanical Press        | Presses ingots into sheets, compacts items              |
   | Mechanical Mixer        | Mixes materials in a Basin (alloys, food, more)         |
   | Millstone               | Grinds materials into powder (wheat to flour, etc.)     |
   | Mechanical Belt         | Transports items and relays rotation sideways           |
   | Deployer               | A robotic arm that "uses" items on blocks or entities   |
   | Chute / Funnel          | Moves items between machines vertically/from sides      |

   **Create addons in this pack:**
   - **Create: Aeronautics + Aeroworks** -- Build actual flying airships!
   - **Create: Big Cannons** -- Functional, physics-based artillery.
   - **Create: New Age** -- Electric power for Create machines.
   - **Create: Diesel Generators** -- Fuel-powered rotation source, great mid-game.
   - **Create: Railways Navigator** -- Full train network with a route planner UI.
   - **Create: Nuclear** -- Late-game nuclear power.
   - **Create: Enchantable Machinery** -- Enchant machines for bonuses.

   > **Pro tip:** Hold any Create item and press **W** (default) to open the **Ponder**
   > system -- an animated in-game tutorial for that exact block. It is one of the
   > best in-game tutorials in any mod. Use it constantly, especially when you are
   > unsure how a machine is supposed to work or be connected.
</details>

## Applied Energistics 2

**What is it?**
Applied Energistics 2 (AE2) is a mass storage and logistics mod. It lets you store
thousands of items across a handful of **Storage Cells** and access everything
through a single searchable **ME Terminal**. Late-game it can automatically craft
almost anything on demand. "ME" stands for Matter Energy.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Your very first step is finding a **Meteorite**. These leave large craters and
      holes in the terrain, so you may have already spotted one. If not, craft a
      **Meteorite Compass** (needs a small amount of Certus Quartz, which spawns
      underground). The Compass points to the nearest meteorite.

      ![1777668285351](image/cubica-intro/1777668285351.png)

   2. Dig into the **center** of the meteorite to find the **Mysterious Cube**. Break it
      to receive all **four Inscriber Presses** -- these are required to craft the
      processors your ME Network needs. Mine the **Certus Quartz Clusters** and any
      **Budding Certus Quartz blocks** you find too (use Silk Touch on Budding blocks --
      without it, they degrade by one tier).

      > **Important:** Never break a **Flawless Budding Certus** block, even with
      > Silk Touch. They cannot be repaired and will degrade. Leave them in place
      > or move them with Spatial Storage later.

   3. Back at your base, place **Growth Accelerators** next to your Budding Certus blocks
      to speed up Certus crystal growth dramatically. Once you have enough Certus, make
      **Fluix Crystals** by throwing Charged Certus Quartz, Redstone Dust, and Nether
      Quartz into a pool of water together.

      ![1777668371933](image/cubica-intro/1777668371933.png)

   4. Craft an **Inscriber** and use your four Presses to make the three processor types
      (Logic, Calculation, Engineering). You now have everything you need for your first
      ME system.

   5. Build a minimal first network:
      - **ME Controller** -- the brain of the network (needs power)
      - **Energy Acceptor** -- converts power from Create or IE into ME power
      - **ME Drive** -- holds Storage Cells
      - **ME Terminal** -- your access point; place on a cable connected to the above

      ![1777668404558](image/cubica-intro/1777668404558.png)

   **Key AE2 concepts:**

   | Term              | What it means                                              |
   |-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
   | Storage Cell      | A card that slots into an ME Drive; holds items digitally  |
   | Channels          | Each cable carries a limited number of connected devices   |
   | ME Controller     | Required for networks larger than 8 devices               |
   | Autocrafting      | Set up Patterns so the network crafts items for you        |
   | P2P Tunnel        | Routes channels cleanly through walls for big networks     |

   **Wiki:** [https://guide.appliedenergistics.org/1.21.1/getting-started](https://guide.appliedenergistics.org/1.21.1/getting-started)

   > **Pro tip:** Start with just a Controller, Energy Acceptor, Drive, and Terminal.
   > Do not worry about autocrafting yet -- even a basic searchable inventory for your
   > whole base is a massive quality of life upgrade. A **4k Storage Cell** as your
   > first cell covers most of early-mid game needs.

</details>

## Immersive Engineering

**What is it?**
Immersive Engineering (IE) adds a retro-industrial feel with **multi-block machines**
-- structures built from multiple placed blocks rather than a single machine block.
Everything in IE is large and physical: hanging power wires, a coke oven puffing
smoke, a crusher taller than your player. It pairs beautifully with Create for a
complete industrial base.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Craft the **Engineer's Manual** first -- a Book combined with a Lever. This
      in-game guide has detailed instructions and 3D blueprints for every machine in
      the mod. It is your most important item. Keep it in your hotbar.

   2. Craft the **Engineer's Hammer** (2 Iron Ingots + 2 Sticks + 1 String). This tool
      is required to **form** every multi-block structure. After placing all the blocks
      for a machine, right-click the correct face with the Hammer to assemble it.

   3. Your first multi-block is the **Coke Oven** -- a 3x3x3 cube of Coke Bricks.
      It takes Coal and turns it into **Coal Coke** (a superior fuel) and produces
      **Creosote Oil** as a byproduct. Creosote Oil is required to craft **Treated Wood**,
      which is used in almost every IE machine.

      <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
      <!-- Screenshot: The Coke Oven multi-block assembled and running, with smoke particles -->
      <!-- Source: https://ftb.fandom.com/wiki/Coke_Oven_(Immersive_Engineering) -->
      ![Immersive Engineering Coke Oven](PLACEHOLDER_ie_coke_oven.png)

   4. Build the **Crude Blast Furnace** next (also a multi-block) to produce **Steel**
      from Iron and Coal Coke. Steel is needed for almost all mid- and late-game IE
      machines. Then upgrade it to an **Improved Blast Furnace** once you have a bit
      of Steel, which allows automated inputs and outputs via hoppers.

   5. IE uses its own power system called **Immersive Flux (IF)**. The easiest early
      power source is the **Kinetic Dynamo**, which converts Create rotational force
      directly into IF. Connect it to your machines via **Wire Coils** strung between
      **Connectors**.

      <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
      <!-- Screenshot: IE wire coils strung between two connectors on machines -->
      <!-- Source: https://www.minecraft-guides.com/mod/immersive-engineering/ -->
      ![IE Wire Coils Between Machines](PLACEHOLDER_ie_wire_coils.png)

   **Key IE items and machines:**

   | Machine / Item     | What it does                                             |
   |--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
   | Coke Oven          | Converts Coal into Coal Coke + Creosote Oil              |
   | Blast Furnace      | Smelts Iron + Coal Coke into Steel                       |
   | Crusher            | Grinds ores into grit -- doubles your ore yield          |
   | Arc Furnace        | High-end bulk smelter for alloys and high throughput     |
   | Kinetic Dynamo     | Converts Create rotation into IE power                   |
   | Wire Coils         | Visual, physical power cables between machines           |
   | Engineer's Revolver| A powerful and moddable gun weapon                       |
   | Railgun            | Late-game energy weapon with extreme range and power     |
   | Conveyor Belt      | IE's own horizontal item transport system                |

   > **Pro tip:** If a multi-block refuses to form when you hit it with the Hammer, open
   > the Engineer's Manual and look at the 3D blueprint for that machine. Check block
   > orientation -- many machines must face a specific direction to work, and the Hammer
   > can rotate them before forming. This solves 95% of "why won't it work" situations.
</details>

# Food and Farming Mods

Eating in this pack is way more interesting than vanilla. The food mods add hundreds
of recipes, and Mystical Agriculture turns farming into a full progression system
for getting resources passively over time.

---

## Farmer's Delight

**What is it?**
Farmer's Delight is the food foundation of this pack. It adds a proper cooking
workflow with dedicated workstations, over twenty new crops, and meals that give
strong status effect buffs. Many other mods in this pack (Cultural Delights, Ocean's
Delight, Vinery, and many more) add their own recipes on top of Farmer's Delight's
cooking system.

> Follow the in-game **Advancements** for Farmer's Delight as your main progression
> guide. Press **L** (default) to open the Advancement screen and look for the
> Farmer's Delight tab. The advancements walk you through the mod step by step!

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Find **Wild Crops** in the world first -- they spawn in various biomes and
      sometimes in loot chests. Wild Cabbage, Tomatoes, Onions, and Rice are the ones
      you need to find once to start farming them. You can also find seeds in
      village chests and shipwrecks.

   2. Craft a **Knife** (any ingot + a stick in the crafting grid, vertically). The
      Knife is your primary Farmer's Delight tool. It gives a guaranteed secondary drop
      when killing mobs (guaranteed leather from cows, guaranteed feathers from chickens,
      etc.) -- very useful even before you start cooking.

   3. Craft a **Cutting Board** (4 Planks + 2 Sticks). Place it down and right-click
      it with an ingredient in your hand to place it on the board, then right-click
      with the appropriate tool to process it. Many cooking recipes require **cut** or
      **chopped** versions of ingredients that you can only make here.

      <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
      <!-- Screenshot: A Cutting Board with a Tomato on it, being sliced with a Knife -->
      <!-- Source: https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/wiki -->
      ![Farmer's Delight Cutting Board](PLACEHOLDER_fd_cutting_board.png)

   4. Craft a **Stove** (4 Bricks + 3 Iron Ingots + a Campfire). The Stove is an
      upgrade to the campfire that can support up to four cooking pots at once and cooks
      six items at a time on its grill surface.

   5. Craft a **Cooking Pot** (5 Iron Ingots + Water Bucket + 2 Bricks + Wood Shovel)
      and place it on top of your Stove (or a Campfire, Lava, or Magma Block). Open it
      and place up to six ingredients inside, then put a **Bowl** in the output slot.
      When heated, it will start cooking. Most soups and stews come out in bowls.

      <!-- IMAGE PLACEHOLDER -->
      <!-- Screenshot: Cooking Pot placed on a Stove, bubbling, with a bowl in the output -->
      <!-- Source: https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/wiki -->
      ![Farmer's Delight Cooking Pot](PLACEHOLDER_fd_cooking_pot.png)

   **Key status effects from food:**

   | Effect          | What it does                                                  |
   |-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
   | Nourishment     | Prevents hunger and saturation loss; enables slow regeneration |
   | Comfort         | Removes Slowness, Hunger, and Weakness temporarily            |
   | Regeneration    | Restores health over time (great before combat)               |
   | Strength        | Increases attack damage                                        |
   | Resistance      | Reduces incoming damage (excellent before a dungeon dive)     |

   **Farmer's Delight addons in this pack:**
   Use **EMI** (press **E** in your inventory to open the recipe viewer) to browse every
   available recipe. There are hundreds of dishes across Cultural Delights, Ocean's
   Delight, Aquaculture Delight, Barbeques Delight, Chef's Delight, Brewin and Chewin
   (drinks!), Vinery, and many more. Explore freely!

   > **Pro tip:** Food buffs stack. A good pre-dungeon meal combo -- a dish with
   > Resistance and one with Regeneration -- can make the difference in a boss fight.
   > Cook in bulk: the Cooking Pot holds multiple servings, and most meals stack well.
</details>

## Mystical Agriculture

**What is it?**
Mystical Agriculture lets you grow almost any resource as a crop -- ores, mob drops,
gemstones, and even materials like Diamond and Netherite. It replaces grinding with
patient, scalable farming. A fully developed Mystical Agriculture setup passively
supplies your base with resources around the clock.

> Mystical Agriculture crops do NOT need water or light! They just need the right
> Essence Farmland and enough space above them.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Go mining for the two starting materials:
      - **Prosperity Ore** (between Y -60 and Y 24) -- drops Prosperity Shards
      - **Inferium Ore** (common underground) -- drops Inferium Essence

      You can also get Inferium Essence from killing any hostile mob (about a 20%
      drop chance per kill), so your early adventuring will naturally build up a supply.

   2. Craft **Inferium Seeds** and plant them on regular Farmland to start. For better
      yields, right-click on regular Farmland with an Inferium Essence to convert it
      to **Inferium Essence Farmland**. Each tier of Essence Farmland increases the
      Inferium dropped per harvest by 50%.

      ![1777668531430](image/cubica-intro/1777668531430.png)

   3. To upgrade Inferium Essence to the next tier, use an **Infusion Crystal**. Combine
      four of the lower tier into one of the higher tier. The full tier ladder is:
      **Inferium > Prudentium > Tertium > Imperium > Supremium**.

   4. To craft Resource Seeds (Iron Seeds, Diamond Seeds, etc.), build an
      **Infusion Altar** with **8 Infusion Pedestals** around it. Place the Altar in
      the world and it will show you exactly where the Pedestals go. Power the recipe
      with a Redstone signal. Seeds require a Prosperity Shard Seed Base, the matching
      tier Essence, and a piece of the resource you want to grow.

      ![1777668569461](image/cubica-intro/1777668569461.png)

   5. Plant Resource Seeds on Essence Farmland of the matching tier. Place
      **Growth Accelerators** underneath the farmland to speed up growth -- they stack,
      so more accelerators means faster crops.

   **Essence tier overview:**

   | Tier        | Seeds you can grow                                      |
   |-------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
   | Inferium    | The base -- gather this first from ore and mobs         |
   | Prudentium  | Stone, Dirt, Wood, Water seeds                          |
   | Tertium     | Iron, Copper, Coal, Flint seeds                         |
   | Imperium    | Gold, Lapis, Redstone, Glowstone seeds                  |
   | Supremium   | Diamond, Emerald, Netherite, Nether Star seeds!         |

   **Late-game bonus:** Craft **Essence Armor** and **Essence Tools** from the tiers
   above. A full set of Supremium Armor lets you fly. You can further upgrade the gear
   with **Augments** on a **Tinkering Table** for effects like Absorption hearts,
   Jump Boost, Fire Resistance, and more.

   **Wiki:** [https://blakesmods.com/wiki/mysticalagriculture/guides/getting-started](https://blakesmods.com/wiki/mysticalagriculture/guides/getting-started)

   > **Pro tip:** Mystical Agriculture is a long-term investment. Plant a small patch
   > of Inferium Seeds early, then ignore it. By mid-game you will have enough essence
   > to start making resource seeds. It works brilliantly with a Create Mechanical
   > Harvester on a moving contraption for a fully automated harvest.
</details>

## The Aether

**What is it?**
The Aether is one of Minecraft's most iconic mods -- a sky dimension that is the
complete opposite of the Nether. It is bright, floating, and full of islands, alien
mobs, and three unique dungeons each with their own boss. Think of it as a parallel
mid-game adventure path alongside the Nether.

![1777667980597](image/cubica-intro/1777667980597.png)

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. You need **Glowstone blocks** first (found in the Nether ceiling, or buy from
      Piglin traders). Build a portal frame the same shape as a Nether portal, but
      use **Glowstone blocks** instead of Obsidian.

      ![1777667917983](image/cubica-intro/1777667917983.png)

   2. Light it with a **bucket of water** (right-click the inside of the frame).
      The portal turns blue and shimmery. Step through!

   3. A few crucial things to know immediately upon arrival:
      - **Regular torches and lanterns cannot be placed in the Aether.** Use
      **Ambrosium Torches** (Ambrosium Shard over a Skyroot Stick) instead.
      - **Vanilla tools mine Aether blocks as slowly as your bare hand.** Craft
      **Skyroot tools** immediately from Skyroot Logs (the local wood type).
      - **Falling off the edge sends you back to the Overworld**, spawning high above
      your portal. Put water around your portal exit before exploring near edges!
      - The Aether has **Eternal Day** until you defeat the final dungeon boss.
      You cannot sleep in beds until then.

   4. Gather resources in this order:
      - **Skyroot** (the local wood) for tools and early crafting
      - **Holystone** (the local stone) for better tools
      - **Ambrosium Shards** (from Ambrosium Ore -- the Aether's coal) for torches and fuel
      - **Zanite Gemstones** (from Zanite Ore -- equivalent to Iron) for strong tools
      - **Gravitite Ore** (the top-tier material; floats upward when mined -- catch it!)


   5. Tame a **Moa** as your flying mount! Moa eggs can be found and hatched in the
      **Incubator** (an Aether utility block). Moas can jump multiple times mid-air,
      making navigation across the floating islands much safer. With the
      **Protect Your Moa** addon in this pack, your Moa has extra protection options.

   **Dungeon progression (in order):**

   | Dungeon        | Boss            | Key tip                                         |
   |----------------|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------|
   | Bronze Dungeon | The Slider      | Cannot take sword damage! Use a pickaxe or axe  |
   | Silver Dungeon | Valkyrie Queen  | Kill 10 Valkyries first to earn Victory Medals  |
   | Gold Dungeon   | The Sun Spirit  | Reflect his white orbs back at him to deal damage|

   > **Pro tip:** Look for ore on the underside of islands! Build scaffolding down to
   > a Cold Aercloud (white cloud block) and look upward -- rare ores like Zanite and
   > Gravitite often generate there, visible from below. It is safer and faster than
   > digging straight down.
</details>

## Deeper and Darker

**What is it?**
Deeper and Darker expands the vanilla Deep Dark biome and adds an entirely new
dimension behind it: **The Otherside**. If you found Ancient Cities exciting, this
mod extends that experience significantly -- new mobs, new loot, new biomes, and
a full gear progression tied to the Warden and the sculk theme.

> The Otherside is **late-game content**. You need to defeat the Warden first.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Find the **Deep Dark** biome (below Y=0, often under mountains or plains). You
      will know it by the sculk carpet covering the floor and the oppressive dark.
      Ancient Cities generate within it.

   2. To open the portal to the Otherside, you need the **Heart of the Deep** -- a
      guaranteed drop from killing the **Warden**. This is intentional: the Otherside
      is designed as post-Warden content.

   3. Inside an Ancient City, find the **Reinforced Deepslate portal frame** at the
      center. Clear any Sculk blocks from the frame surface (they prevent activation).
      Right-click the frame with the Heart of the Deep to ignite the portal.
      The Heart is not consumed -- you can light multiple portals with one Heart.

      ![1777668714522](image/cubica-intro/1777668714522.png)

      > **Critical:** Before entering the Otherside, write down your portal coordinates!
      > If you lose the portal, the only way back to the Overworld is to die.

   4. The Otherside applies a permanent **Darkness effect** to all players. The only
      way to remove it is to wear a **Warden Helmet** (crafted by upgrading Netherite
      Helmet with a Warden Upgrade Smithing Template, which drops from Ancient City
      chests at about 60% spawn rate). You will struggle badly without it.

   5. The Otherside has four biomes with distinct feels and dangers:

   | Biome            | What is it like                                          |
   |------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
   | Deeplands        | Most dangerous; open Sculk terrain, high mob density      |
   | Echoing Forest   | Purple Echo Trees light the area; mostly Shattered mobs  |
   | Blooming Caverns | Bloom wood and Angler Fish; less hostile                  |
   | Overcast Columns | Tall pillars; fog-heavy; Phantom swarms                  |

   6. Explore the **Ancient Temple** in the Deeplands for the best loot. It has three
      levels, each with better rewards. Break Ancient Vases carefully -- some may summon
      the Ancient Lurker miniboss.

   **Key Deeper and Darker rewards:**

   | Item                   | What it does                                           |
   |------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
   | Warden Armor Set       | Upgraded from Netherite; negates Darkness effect       |
   | Soul Elytra            | A boosted Elytra that randomly boosts without rockets  |
   | Sculk Transmitter      | Redstone-like wireless signaling device                |
   | Sculk Bones            | Dropped by Sculk Centipedes; needed for Soul Elytra    |
   | Soul Dust              | Dropped by the Shattered mob; needed for Soul Elytra   |
   | Soul Crystal           | Dropped by the Stalker; needed for Soul Elytra         |

   > **Warning:** The Warden still spawns in the Otherside when Sculk Shriekers are
   > triggered. Sneak constantly, move slowly, and keep noise to a minimum -- the same
   > rules as the Deep Dark still apply, but now with worse visibility and more mobs.

   **Wiki:** [https://www.minecraft-guides.com/wiki/deeper-and-darker/(https://www.minecraft-guides.com/wiki/deeper-and-darker/)]
</details>

## The Undergarden

**What is it?**
The Undergarden is a vast underground dimension sitting below the bedrock layer.
Unlike the Nether's hostile fire and lava, the Undergarden is lush, dark, and dripping
with eerie atmosphere -- alien mushroom forests, poisonous bogs, glowing plants, and
15 distinct biomes. It has its own four-tier ore progression and is a great exploration
destination for mid-game players looking for something different.

<details>
   <summary><ins>I want to know more</ins></summary>

   **How to get started:**

   1. Craft a **Catalyst** -- the item used to activate the Undergarden portal.
      It is crafted from Gold Ingots, Iron Ingots, and a Diamond.

      ![1777668812811](image/cubica-intro/1777668812811.png)

   2. Build a portal frame the same shape as a Nether portal but using **Stone Bricks**
      or **Deepslate Bricks** instead of Obsidian.

   3. Right-click the **bottom inner block** of the frame with the Catalyst to activate
      the portal. Step through!

      ![1777668830108](image/cubica-intro/1777668830108.png)

   4. The Undergarden has four tiers of ore and gear, in progression order:

      - **Cloggrum** -- the first ore tier; smelt raw Cloggrum and craft tools and armor.
      Cloggrum tools are comparable to Diamond. Note: Cloggrum Sticks (from local
      trees) are required for Cloggrum tool crafting instead of regular Sticks.
      - **Froststeel** -- requires a Cloggrum Pickaxe to mine. Tools and weapons have
      a **chill effect** that slows enemies when you hit them.
      - **Utherium** -- top standard ore tier with excellent stats.
      - **Forgotten** -- the rarest and most powerful tier. Forgotten gear is obtained
      from rare **Catacombs** structures and enemies inside them, not from regular ore.

   5. Watch out for **Virulent Mix** -- pools of poisonous water scattered through the
      dimension that look similar to regular water. Swimming in them deals damage. Avoid
      standing bodies of liquid unless you are certain of what they are.

   **Key Undergarden items:**

   | Item              | What it does                                              |
   |-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
   | Cloggrum Ingot    | First tier metal; tools are Diamond-comparable            |
   | Froststeel Ingot  | Second tier; weapons apply a chill/slow to enemies        |
   | Utherium Ingot    | Third tier; top standard gear                             |
   | Forgotten Ingot   | Rare fourth tier; obtained from Catacomb enemies          |
   | Catalyst          | Activates the portal to the Undergarden                   |
   | Cloggrum Bucket   | A bucket variant that works with Undergarden liquids      |

   > **Pro tip:** The Undergarden has no day/night cycle and mobs spawn constantly.
   > Light up your surroundings as you go and keep moving -- staying still in the dark
   > is a death sentence. Bring plenty of food: the dimension has its own food items
   > (Underbean on a Stick, Gloomgourd Pie) that are worth collecting, but you will
   > want a solid supply from the Overworld for your first visit.
</details>
